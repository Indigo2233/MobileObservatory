MJSol'Ex
=========

The Android build links the Apache-2.0 MJSol'Ex Rust reconstruction core at:

https://github.com/Indigo2233/mjsolex-rs
Revision: 2b047d9e7f87b28c0800dadeafebd94b08544552 (v0.0.3)

Indigo Observatory adds a JNI bridge for file input, progress, cancellation and
output paths. The reconstruction, spectrum fitting, corrections and product
encoding execute in the upstream Rust crate.

MJSol'Ex SER layout and reconstruction are in turn based on Astro4j and
JSol'Ex revision 7ffdaf5c.
