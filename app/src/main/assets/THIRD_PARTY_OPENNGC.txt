catalog/deepsky.csv is a trimmed subset of the OpenNGC database (NGC.csv and
addendum.csv), keeping identifiers, object type, J2000 coordinates, magnitude
and major axis for offline star map search. Chinese aliases for well-known
objects are added by this project. Regenerate with
scripts/generate_deepsky_catalog.py.
