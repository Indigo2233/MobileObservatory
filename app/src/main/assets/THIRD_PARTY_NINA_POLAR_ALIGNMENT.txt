N.I.N.A. Three Point Polar Alignment plugin

Project: https://bitbucket.org/Isbeorn/nina.plugin.polaralignment
License: Mozilla Public License 2.0

The Android three-point polar-alignment calculator is an independent Kotlin
implementation derived from the geometry and validation approach used by the
N.I.N.A. plugin. The Android UI, ASTAP integration, and app workflow are
project-specific.
